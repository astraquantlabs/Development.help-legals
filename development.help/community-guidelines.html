Community Guidelines

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

These Community Guidelines help ensure Development.Help remains a safe, respectful, and welcoming place for everyone.

1. Respect Everyone

  • Treat all members with respect.
  • No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination.
  • Keep discussions civil and constructive.

2. Keep the Community Safe

  • No scams, phishing, malware, or malicious links.
  • Do not attempt to compromise another user's account or privacy.
  • Report suspicious activity to the staff.

3. No Spam

  • Do not spam messages, emojis, or mentions.
  • Do not flood channels with repeated content.
  • Use channels for their intended purpose.

4. Advertising

  • No self-promotion or advertising without staff permission.
  • Do not send unsolicited direct messages to members.

5. Appropriate Content

  • No illegal, harmful, or abusive content.
  • No NSFW or explicit content.
  • Respect intellectual property and copyright laws.

6. Staff Decisions

Staff members enforce these guidelines to maintain a healthy community. If you disagree with a moderation action, you may respectfully appeal through the appropriate support channel.

7. Reporting Violations

If you witness a violation of these guidelines, report it to a moderator or through the official support system instead of engaging in arguments.

8. Changes to These Guidelines

Development.Help may update these Community Guidelines at any time. Continued participation in the community indicates acceptance of the updated guidelines.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Development.Help collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Discord server, website, bots, and related services.

3. How We Use Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared when required by law or when necessary to operate trusted third-party services used by Development.Help.

Information may be stored securely for as long as necessary to operate the server, provide support, enforce our rules, or comply with legal obligations.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

  • Request information about stored data.

  • Request deletion of eligible stored data, subject to legal or operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect information from users who do not meet that requirement.

# 📜 Development.Help Community Rulebook

**Effective Date:** July 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 25, 2026

---

## Introduction

Welcome to **Development.Help**.

These rules are designed to maintain a safe, respectful, professional, and collaborative environment for everyone. By joining or participating in this community, you agree to comply with this Rulebook, our Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and Privacy Policy.

Failure to follow these rules may result in moderation actions, including warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans.

---

# Section I — General Conduct

### Article 1
No spamming or flooding channels with repeated messages, emojis, GIFs, stickers, or reactions.

### Article 2
Treat all members with respect regardless of their background, experience, or opinions.

### Article 3
Harassment, bullying, threats, or intimidation of any member is strictly prohibited.

### Article 4
Hate speech, discrimination, racism, sexism, or any form of prejudice will not be tolerated.

### Article 5
Keep discussions civil and avoid starting unnecessary arguments or drama.

### Article 6
Follow instructions given by moderators and administrators at all times.

### Article 7
Respect the decisions made by staff. Appeals may be submitted through the proper process.

### Article 8
Use common sense when interacting with other members.

### Article 9
Do not intentionally disrupt conversations or community activities.

### Article 10
English is the primary language unless a channel specifically allows another language.

---

# Section II — Chat Rules

### Article 11
Keep conversations relevant to the purpose of each channel.

### Article 12
Avoid excessive use of CAPITAL LETTERS.

### Article 13
Do not abuse emojis, stickers, GIFs, or reactions.

### Article 14
Do not excessively mention or ping members, roles, or staff.

### Article 15
Ghost pinging or fake mentioning members is prohibited.

### Article 16
Avoid posting meaningless, repetitive, or low-effort messages.

### Article 17
Do not send chain messages or misleading announcements.

### Article 18
Respect slowmode and other channel restrictions.

### Article 19
Keep discussions appropriate for all audiences.

### Article 20
Do not intentionally derail active discussions.

---

# Section III — Content Policy

### Article 21
NSFW, pornographic, or sexually explicit content is strictly prohibited.

### Article 22
Graphic violence, gore, or disturbing media is not permitted.

### Article 23
Do not share malware, viruses, malicious scripts, or harmful software.

### Article 24
Phishing links, scams, and fraudulent content are strictly prohibited.

### Article 25
Do not promote illegal activities or distribute copyrighted material without authorization.

---

# Section IV — Privacy & Security

### Article 26
Respect the privacy of all members at all times.

### Article 27
Do not share another person's personal information without their explicit consent.

### Article 28
Doxxing or attempting to reveal private information is strictly prohibited.

### Article 29
Never ask members to share passwords, tokens, API keys, or other confidential credentials.

### Article 30
Report any suspicious accounts, scams, or security concerns to the staff team.

---

# Section V — Advertising & Promotion

### Article 31
Advertising other Discord servers is prohibited unless explicitly approved by staff.

### Article 32
Self-promotion is only permitted in designated channels.

### Article 33
Unsolicited advertising through direct messages to server members is prohibited.

### Article 34
Referral links, affiliate links, or promotional campaigns require prior staff approval.

### Article 35
Repeated promotion of the same content may be treated as spam.

### Article 36
Recruiting members for external communities without permission is prohibited.

### Article 37
Commercial advertisements require authorization from server management.

### Article 38
Partnership requests must follow the partnership guidelines.

### Article 39
Misleading advertisements or deceptive promotions are prohibited.

### Article 40
Staff reserve the right to remove any advertisement that does not benefit the community.

---

# Section VI — Support & Tickets

### Article 41
Open support tickets only for legitimate questions or issues.

### Article 42
Do not create duplicate tickets for the same issue.

### Article 43
Remain patient while waiting for a response from staff.

### Article 44
Do not repeatedly ping support staff inside tickets.

### Article 45
Provide accurate and complete information when requesting assistance.

### Article 46
False reports or fabricated evidence are strictly prohibited.

### Article 47
Abuse of the ticket system may result in restrictions or disciplinary action.

### Article 48
Do not close a ticket until your issue has been resolved unless instructed otherwise.

### Article 49
Respect support staff and cooperate throughout the support process.

### Article 50
Staff may close inactive or resolved tickets without prior notice.

---

# Section VII — Development & Technical Standards

### Article 51
Share code only in the appropriate development channels.

### Article 52
Format code using code blocks whenever possible.

### Article 53
Do not claim ownership of work created by others.

### Article 54
Respect open-source licenses and provide attribution where required.

### Article 55
Do not upload stolen source code or leaked proprietary software.

### Article 56
Keep project discussions constructive and professional.

### Article 57
Report bugs responsibly instead of publicly exploiting them.

### Article 58
Never share bot tokens, API keys, passwords, or other sensitive credentials.

### Article 59
Discussions about cybersecurity must remain legal and educational.

### Article 60
The distribution of malware, token grabbers, ransomware, or other malicious software is strictly prohibited.

### Article 61
Do not intentionally exploit bugs in the server, bots, or website.

### Article 62
Beta features must not be abused or leaked without authorization.

### Article 63
Credit contributors when using collaborative work.

### Article 64
Keep feedback respectful and focused on improvement.

### Article 65
Follow all project-specific guidelines provided by developers or maintainers.

---

# Section VIII — Voice Channels

### Article 66
Be respectful during voice conversations.

### Article 67
Do not intentionally create loud or disruptive noises.

### Article 68
Microphone spam, soundboards, and voice changers must not disturb others.

### Article 69
Do not interrupt or talk over other members excessively.

### Article 70
Recording voice conversations without the consent of all participants is prohibited where applicable.

### Article 71
Respect voice channel permissions and participant limits.

### Article 72
Do not evade voice mutes or moderation actions.

### Article 73
Follow moderator instructions while in voice channels.

### Article 74
Keep voice discussions appropriate for all members.

### Article 75
Staff may disconnect or mute members disrupting voice channels.

---

# Section IX — Moderation & Enforcement

### Article 76
Moderators have the authority to enforce these rules fairly and consistently.

### Article 77
Failure to follow moderator instructions may result in disciplinary action.

### Article 78
Staff decisions are final unless successfully appealed through the official appeal process.

### Article 79
Ban evasion using alternate accounts is strictly prohibited.

### Article 80
Alternate accounts may be restricted or removed if used to violate server rules.

### Article 81
Penalties may include warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on the severity of the violation.

### Article 82
Repeated violations may result in increasingly severe disciplinary actions.

### Article 83
Staff may investigate suspected rule violations before taking action.

### Article 84
Members are expected to cooperate honestly during investigations.

### Article 85
Providing false evidence or intentionally misleading staff is prohibited.

---

# Section X — Appeals & Community Standards

### Article 86
Members may appeal disciplinary actions through the designated appeal system.

### Article 87
Appeals must be respectful, truthful, and supported by relevant information.

### Article 88
Submitting multiple duplicate appeals may result in their closure without review.

### Article 89
Abuse of the appeal process may result in additional disciplinary action.

### Article 90
Staff reserve the right to deny appeals that lack sufficient evidence or violate community standards.

---

# Section XI — Final Provisions

### Article 91
All members must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

### Article 92
These rules apply to usernames, display names, avatars, biographies, custom statuses, and all server activity.

### Article 93
These rules apply across all text channels, voice channels, threads, forums, events, and support tickets.

### Article 94
The server administration reserves the right to remove any content that negatively affects the community.

### Article 95
These rules may be updated, modified, or expanded at any time without prior notice.

### Article 96
It is each member's responsibility to stay informed about the latest version of this Rulebook.

### Article 97
Ignorance of these rules does not exempt any member from enforcement.

### Article 98
By joining or remaining in this community, you agree to comply with all published rules and policies.

### Article 99
Any behavior that threatens the safety, integrity, or reputation of the community may result in immediate action, even if not explicitly listed in this Rulebook.

### Article 100
The mission of **Development.Help** is to maintain a safe, respectful, welcoming, and professional environment where members can learn, collaborate, and grow together.

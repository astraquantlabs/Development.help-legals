# 🤝 Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** July 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 25, 2026

---

## Welcome

These Community Guidelines help ensure **Development.Help** remains a safe, respectful, and welcoming community for everyone. By participating in our community, you agree to follow these guidelines.

---

## 1. Respect Everyone

All members deserve to be treated with respect.

- Be polite and constructive.
- No harassment, bullying, threats, or discrimination.
- Avoid personal attacks and unnecessary arguments.
- Respect differences in opinions, backgrounds, and experience levels.

---

## 2. Keep the Community Safe

Help us maintain a secure environment.

- Do not share malware, phishing links, scams, or malicious software.
- Never attempt to compromise another member's account, privacy, or security.
- Report suspicious behavior to the staff immediately.

---

## 3. No Spam

Keep conversations organized and relevant.

- Do not spam messages, emojis, GIFs, or mentions.
- Avoid repeated or excessive posting.
- Use each channel for its intended purpose.
- Do not intentionally disrupt discussions.

---

## 4. Advertising & Self-Promotion

Advertising is restricted.

- No self-promotion without staff approval.
- No unsolicited advertisements.
- Do not send promotional direct messages to members.
- Referral links and excessive promotions are prohibited unless approved.

---

## 5. Appropriate Content

Keep content suitable for all members.

- No illegal or harmful content.
- No NSFW, explicit, or sexually suggestive content.
- No hateful or abusive material.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property rights.
